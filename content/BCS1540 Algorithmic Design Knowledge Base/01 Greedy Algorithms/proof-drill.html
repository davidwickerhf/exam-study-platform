Greedy Proof Drill

Three levels of difficulty. Start with Level 1 (fill in blanks), graduate to Level 2 (write from memory), finish with Level 3 (theory questions). Dijkstra excluded.

Level 1. The proof structure is given. Fill in the key phrases from memory (or guesswork). Click "Reveal model" to see what each blank should contain. Work through all 4 styles.

Level 2. No template scaffold. Read the problem, then write the proof from scratch in the box. Click "Reveal model proof" only after you've written something — even a partial attempt is more valuable than reading the model cold.
Level 3. Conceptual questions about proof selection and structure. Click an option, then "Check" to see if you're right and read the explanation.
