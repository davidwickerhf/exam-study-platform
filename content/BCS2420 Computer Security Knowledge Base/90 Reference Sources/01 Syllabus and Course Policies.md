# BCS2420 Computer Security Syllabus and Policies

## Course Code and Title
- Programme: BSc Computer Science
- Study Year: Jan-March, 2026
- Period: 4
- ECTS: 4

## Course Objectives
The central aim of BCS2420 Computer Security is to provide you with a solid foundation in the principles and practices of securing computer systems. Building upon the fundamental concepts you've encountered in your computer science courses, this course delves into the crucial aspects of protecting digital assets from unauthorized access and malicious activities. Both theoretical concepts discussed in the lectures and hands-on practice in the labs will equip you to navigate the complex landscape of computer security.

By the end of this course, you should be able to:
- Explain core computer security concepts, services, and mechanisms introduced in lectures.
- Describe various aspects of physical, operational, and organizational security and their roles in a comprehensive security strategy.
- Analyze the critical role of human factors in the security of computer systems.
- Understand fundamental principles of cryptography (symmetric, asymmetric) and the role of Public Key Infrastructure (PKI).
- Apply techniques for hardening computing systems to reduce vulnerabilities.
- Compare different security approaches and technologies used to address specific threats.
- Communicate effectively about computer security concepts using appropriate terminology.
- Critically evaluate the security posture of a given system or application.

## Course Philosophy
- The course emphasizes theory plus hands-on labs.
- Labs are intended to develop the habit of identifying, analyzing, and mitigating threats rather than blindly following commands.
- The lectures provide the conceptual compass; the labs provide the practical terrain.

## Course Materials
- No required textbook.
- Focus on lecture material, labs, and official documentation for tools.
- Kali Linux or a comparable security distribution is expected for practical work.
- Key tools explicitly mentioned by the syllabus include `nmap`, Wireshark, and metasploit.

## Study Load
- 4 ECTS = 112 hours total.
- 34 contact hours.
- Approximately 10 examinable topics.

## Ethics and Support
- Use security tools ethically and legally.
- Only test systems you own or have explicit permission to test.
- Support is available through office hours, Discord, and email.

## Assessment and Grades
- Project: 25 points (25%)
- Final Exam: 75 points (75%)
- You must pass the final exam for the project grade to count.

Grade mapping:
- 10: 95-100%
- 9: 85-94%
- 8: 75-84%
- 7: 65-74%
- 6: 55-64%
- F: <55

## Final Exam
- Closed-book
- 120 minutes
- Only writing instruments and a DACS-approved calculator

## Resit
If you do not pass the regular exam, you may take a resit. The project grade from the normal period still counts. The final grade is:

`max((resit exam out of 7.5) + (project out of 2.5), (resit exam out of 10))`

## Late Work and Honesty
- Project deliverables submitted late incur a penalty of 2 points per day.
- Violations of academic honesty can result in a failing grade and further disciplinary action.
