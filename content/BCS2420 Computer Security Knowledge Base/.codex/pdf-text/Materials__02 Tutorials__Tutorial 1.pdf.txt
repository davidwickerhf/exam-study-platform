   application that handles critical financial transactions.
     – Threat probability, T : 0.02 (2% chance of occurring

   (a) Calculate the annual risk R using the equation: R = T ×
   V ×C
   (b) Briefly discuss two ways the organization might reduce

   0.02.
     – Threat probability (original): T = 0.1
